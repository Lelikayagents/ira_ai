draught-animal which had been utilized for more than the previously agreed purpose; he may even have given the original case 
which has come down to us by chance through Valerius Maximus

order to drive to Aricia (in the Alban Hills); he drove, however,  
through the City and on to a hill on its far side, and was therefore 
found guilty of furtum.
