For some conceptions there are terms, but no definitions.  
This lack can hardly be due to cuts on the part of the  
Compilers, as the Byzantines, who inclined to pedagogic  
and pedantic fashions, were much attached to definitions; 
their Digest title de verkomm signification  (50. 16) alone  
would be a proof of this.
Gaius, as may be seen from his institutions,  seldom makes use of 
a definition (cf., e.g., iii. 89, 90, 139, 142, 148, 155). There is, to 
take an example, no definition of the important and intricate conception ‘actio’. The fourth book, dedicated to the actiones,  begins 
straightway with the genera actionum,  just as the description of
