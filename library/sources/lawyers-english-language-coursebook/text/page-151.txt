AN EMPLOYMENT CLAIM

Exercise 1

If you want to make a claim at an employment tribunal you must check that the nature of your claim is
covered by the tribunal's jurisdiction list. On the next page you will see ten types of claim that are covered by
the jurisdiction list. Look at the employees’ complaints below. Match each employee with the correct type

of complaint.
