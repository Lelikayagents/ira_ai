If these manifestations of fidelity in Roman life are kept  
in mind, many phenomena of Roman law explain themselves. No longer is one surprised at the paucity of the  
available legal material relating to associations with legal  
personality or to contracts of labour, or at the detailed and  
loving treatment accorded to the mandate and the negotiorum gestio.  Association and labour contracts were of
