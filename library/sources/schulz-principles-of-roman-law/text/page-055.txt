The post-classical era did not greatly improve on these  
attempts at systematization. The system of Gains9  Insti-  
tutiones was retained with additions in Pseudo-Ulpian’s  
liber singularis2  and also forms the base of Justinian’s  
institutions.  The sententiae of the Pseudo-Paulus,4 the  
collections of constitutions in the Codices Gregorianus,  
Hermogenianus, Theodosianus, and Justinianus5  and also  
Justinian’s Digest6, were based on the Digest system,  
though this was everywhere augmented by new insertions  
and digressions.
