acted in the same way. Cicero the Governor in the lawsuit brought by Brutus’ bankers against certain Greek  
cities clearly recognized that the usurious interest charged  
by Brutus was insupportable by the debtor; but Brutus  
was the friend of Atticus,5 and so Cicero’s duty as an  
official battled with his duty as a friend. Not being able  
to find a different way out, he shelved the whole matter  
till the end of his term of office and left the settlement to  
his successor.6 Mommsen’s criticism of Cicero on account
