governors of the senatorial provinces, and local self-  
government. There was never any question in Rome of  
granting equal rights of citizenship, of allowing, in the  
manner demanded by the liberty of Greek democracy, the 
supreme power so to change hands that any one might  
conceivably succeed to it.2
